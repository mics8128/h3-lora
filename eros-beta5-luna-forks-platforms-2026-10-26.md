## Acceptance contract

Only extremely slight numerical differences may be considered, with **no visually/audibly discernible regression in identity, motion, dialogue, voice or lip-sync**. Prefer bitwise-equivalent existing native INT8 output. Keep 4-step ER-SDE/beta57, all 50 layers, reuse/core=1, 768×1344, existing INT8 projections, BF16 video VAE, F32 audio, beta5 BF16 RMSNorm→F32 final semantics. No sparse/local attention, W4A8, layer/token reduction, cross-step KV/residual reuse, or resolution/reference/duration reduction. Such approaches are research-only, not adoption candidates. Video-process 40 GiB is a soft target with modest overshoot acceptable, not permission to consume OS/LLM space. CUDA speedups are not Mac claims. No media generation remains in force.

## Exact streaming / distributed attention versus approximations

**Query streaming**: each Q slice sees every K/V. Rectangular shape controls peak/dispatch, not connectivity. In head-major layout Q subranges are strided across heads: offset-only contiguous slicing is wrong unless repacked or true strides supplied. Full-KV repeated dispatch can be slower and framework allocations may persist.

**Online-softmax K/V streaming**: retain per-query running maximum m, normalizer l and weighted accumulator a; for a new partition with m2,l2,a2, combine at M=max(m,m2): l'=exp(m-M)l+exp(m2-M)l2 and a'=exp(m-M)a+exp(m2-M)a2, output a'/l'. Every key contributes. No score matrix required. Mathematically full dense, **not bitwise** due reduction and storage choices. Scratch O(querytile×headdim), plus input/output/workspace; bandwidth and occupancy still matter.

**Distributed dense attention**: sequence/ring K/V exchange or head/sequence all-to-all can preserve all attention pairs if global normalization and masks are correct. It introduces communication, synchronization, partition-dependent FP reductions and often different kernels. LightX2V SP/TP recipes establish a CUDA distributed execution option, not verified Apple multi-GPU implementation or40GiB footprint. Current single unified-memory GPU gains no free parallel hardware from a distributed algorithm. No RingAttention source/commit was inspected here; do not claim a ready portable implementation.

**Approximate VSA/sparse/local** removes edges. **Sage** typically quantizes attention arithmetic; existing INT8 *projections* do not imply quantized attention is neutral. **Cross-step cache/reuse** uses hidden state from a different latent/sigma. All are excluded adoption under this contract. Weight-cache streaming and timestep-only AdaLN are distinct and must not be mislabeled cross-step target KV reuse.
